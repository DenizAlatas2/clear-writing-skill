# German and English examples

These examples are invented. They illustrate decisions, not fixed templates. Quoted inputs are material to revise and may contain wording or punctuation excluded from the output.

## DE: Neue Kundenantwort, Sie

Briefing: A customer has received a damaged lamp. Support can arrange collection after receiving a photo. Support will give an update by Tuesday at 12:00. There is no confirmed replacement date.

> Es tut uns leid, dass die Lampe beschädigt angekommen ist. Bitte schicken Sie uns ein Foto des Schadens. Danach können wir die Abholung organisieren. Wir melden uns bis Dienstag um 12 Uhr mit einem Update. Einen Termin für den Ersatz können wir Ihnen noch nicht nennen.

The update deadline remains a commitment. It does not become a replacement deadline. The apology responds to the reported problem without claiming personal feelings.

## DE: Überarbeiten ohne stärkere Behauptung

Input:

> Die ersten Messungen sind vielversprechend. Es gibt jedoch keine Evidenz dafür, dass die neue Variante bei jeder Last schneller ist. Im Test mit 200 Anfragen lag die mittlere Antwortzeit über 30 Prozent niedriger, was einen echten Meilenstein darstellt.

Revision:

> Im Test mit 200 Anfragen lag die mittlere Antwortzeit der neuen Variante über 30 Prozent niedriger. Ob sie bei jeder Last schneller ist, lässt sich aus den ersten Messungen nicht sagen.

The comparison keeps its quantifier and test scope. The remaining uncertainty follows from the stated measurements. No new experiment or missing-data claim is invented.

## DE: Du und fachliche Genauigkeit

Input:

> Um unsere North Star auf die Straße zu bringen, solltest du im Sinne einer nachhaltigen Optimierung die Token-Rotation implementieren, wobei bestehende Tokens noch 15 Minuten gültig bleiben müssen.

Revision:

> Implementiere die Token-Rotation so, dass bestehende Tokens noch 15 Minuten gültig bleiben.

The required behavior stays explicit. The technical term and the ordinary word hyphen remain. The metaphor contributes no additional instruction here.

## EN: Draft a clear refusal

Briefing: A workshop is full. There is no waiting list. Another session is scheduled for 18 November, with booking opening on 4 November. Reply politely without promising a place.

> This workshop is full, and we do not have a waiting list. The next session is on 18 November. Booking opens on 4 November.

This is a complete answer. A reassurance paragraph would not create another place.

## EN: Preserve uncertainty and responsibility

Input:

> We are delighted to share that the infrastructure team could complete the migration by Friday, provided the vendor approves the access request by Wednesday. This is not proven, but it is our current estimate.

Revision:

> The infrastructure team could complete the migration by Friday if the vendor approves access by Wednesday. That is our current estimate.

The estimate stays tentative. Approval remains a condition. The editor does not turn Friday into a promise.

## EN: Good text, minimal intervention

Input and output:

> Thanks for catching that. I changed the label to “Save draft” and kept the keyboard shortcut.

The wording is already direct and warm. Adding slang, a personal anecdote, or another sentence would not help.

## Protected original material

An exact quotation, command, or URL may contain excluded punctuation. Preserve its exact contents and explain the conflict if the user expects those exclusions everywhere. Offer a clearly labelled paraphrase when an exact quotation is unnecessary. Do not present an altered quotation as the original.
